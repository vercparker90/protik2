JunDubrovnikCroatiaCity walls at golden hourLate afternoon

The shot

Walk the walls with the sun low, then turn the phone toward the orange rooftops and the Adriatic.

Plan it

Go in May, June or September. Enter at opening time to beat the cruise crowds.

MarPetraJordanSiq canyon revealEarly morning

The shot

Film the narrow Siq in a steady walk, ending on the first glimpse of the Treasury.

Plan it

Visit March–May or October–November. Tripods may need permission, so ask at the gate.

AugZanzibarTanzaniaDhow sailing at duskSunset

The shot

Low angle from the beach as a wooden dhow crosses the sun, then cut to Stone Town’s alleys.

Plan it

Dry months are June–October and January–February. Ask boat crews before filming them.

FebLofotenNorwayRed cabins under the auroraNight and blue hour

The shot

Timelapse of green aurora over rorbu cabins, with a warm window light in the foreground.

Plan it

Aurora season runs September–March. Bring spare batteries, the cold drains them fast.

NovJaipurIndiaAmber Fort at sunriseFirst light

The shot

Slow tilt up the fort walls with the sun rising, then a handheld walk through the courtyards.

Plan it

October–March is comfortable. Check camera and drone rules at each monument.

AprUyuniBoliviaMirror salt flatSunrise and sunset

The shot

Walk toward the horizon on the thin water layer so sky and ground merge into one frame.

Plan it

Rainy season, roughly January–April, brings the mirror. Go with a licensed local guide.

OctSeychellesLa DigueGranite boulders and turquoise seaMorning

The shot

Bike along the coast and cut to a wide of Anse Source d’Argent with clear water in front.

Plan it

April–May and October–November are calm. Tide changes the look, so check it before you shoot.

JulFaroe IslandsDenmarkGrass-roof village in the mistSoft overcast

The shot

Start on a waterfall dropping into the sea, then move to a quiet village with turf roofs.

Plan it

June–August has the longest days. Weather changes fast, so keep a rain cover on the phone.

Operator check-in

Tour operators and tourism boards use Gatefilm to turn their itineraries into footage people want to share. Here are the questions we hear most.

What do you deliver?

Location briefs with shot ideas, light timing and access notes, plus day plans built for filming.

Do you run creator trips?

Yes. We design hosted itineraries with clear deliverables and require partnerships to be disclosed in every post.

Can you train our guides?

We run short phone-filming workshops covering hooks, framing, captions and a posting routine your team can keep up.

How fast do you reply?

Within two business days. Send us a destination and a date and we’ll take it from there.
