Privacy

Chroma Stock only collects the information needed to process an order, deliver your files and keep the store running. We do not sell personal data.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name when you place an order or join the newsletter.
  • Order details: the clips purchased, amount paid and delivery status.
  • Basic, aggregated analytics about how the site is used. No advertising profiles.

How it is used

We use order data to deliver download links, provide support and meet tax and accounting obligations. If you subscribe, we use your email to send occasional collection updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who processes it

Payments, email delivery and hosting are handled by established third-party providers acting on our instructions. They receive only the data required to perform those services. We do not share your information for anyone else’s marketing.

Cookies

The site uses essential storage to remember your cart. Analytics, if enabled, are configured to avoid identifying individual visitors. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data by emailing hello@chroma.stock. We respond within 30 days.

Terms of use

These terms cover purchases from Chroma Stock and the license that comes with each clip.

What you are buying

Each purchase grants a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use one clip in commercial and personal finished projects. The price is $19 per clip unless stated otherwise at checkout.

You may

  • Use the footage in advertising, social media, websites, broadcast, film and client work.
  • Edit, grade, crop, loop and composite the footage.
  • Use the same clip in unlimited finished projects, without attribution.

You may not

  • Resell, redistribute or sublicense the raw footage as stock or as a downloadable library.
  • Share the original files with other teams or clients as standalone assets.
  • Claim authorship of the original footage or use it to train generative AI models.

Delivery and refunds

Files are delivered digitally immediately after payment. Because delivery is instant, all sales are final. If a file is corrupt or you receive the wrong clip, contact us and we will replace it or refund the purchase.
